How It Works

  1. Enable credit limit enforcement in your company settings
  2. Set credit limits and policy (Block/Warn) for each customer
  3. On Sales Order or Invoice, Odoo blocks or warns if the limit would be exceeded
  4. Managers in the special group can always bypass

Setup & Configuration

  • Company: Settings → Companies → Credit Limit tab
  • Customer: Contacts → Customer → Accounting tab
  • Group: Assign "Credit Limit Manager" to users who should bypass limits
